# Review frames

## Steel man

Answer each question with a fact, not a hope. Skip any that do not apply.

1. **Need.** What operational problem does the notice describe, in the government's words? Who has the problem: a named command, program office or operator community?
2. **Fit.** Which sentence in the objective does the company's technology answer directly? Quote it.
3. **Edge.** Why this team over a prime or another small business: speed, a fielded prototype, operator access, a data set, a cost structure?
4. **Proof.** What evidence already exists: test results, a pilot, a letter of support, a prior award, a customer using it?
5. **Path.** If this goes well, what comes next: Phase II, a follow-on OTA, a program of record, a commercial market? Name the transition path or say it is missing.
6. **Value of losing.** Even a loss can buy something: TPOC feedback, a debrief, a relationship with the program office. Say what this one buys.

## Red team

Score as a tired evaluator with forty proposals and a rubric. For each evaluation criterion, give 1–5:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Exceeds the requirement with proof the evaluator can cite |
| 4 | Meets it with proof |
| 3 | Meets it on assertion only |
| 2 | Partly meets it, or the evidence is weak |
| 1 | Does not address it |

Then attack:

- **Disqualifiers:** eligibility, format, page limits, required forms, registration (SAM.gov UEI, DSIP account), deadlines. Any one ends the bid.
- **The objective gap:** which part of the topic or notice the company does not address. Evaluators score what is written, not what the team could do.
- **Assertion without proof:** every "we can" with no test, data or citation behind it.
- **Team:** missing roles (PI time, domain expert, manufacturing, security), key people who are part-time or not yet hired.
- **Transition:** commercialization or transition that names no customer, no follow-on vehicle and no money.
- **Competition:** who else bids, the likely incumbent, and why the evaluator would pick them.
- **Price and scope:** a work plan that does not fit the phase's money or period of performance.
- **Opportunity cost:** what the company stops doing for the weeks this takes.

## Color-team reviews

For a draft, match the review to its stage:

| Review | Stage | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Pink team | Outline or storyboard | Does the structure answer every criterion in order? |
| Red team | Full draft | Would it win as written, scored as the evaluator would? |
| Gold team | Final | Is it compliant, priced right, and worth submitting? |
